## Abstract

EgoSteer builds language-guided dexterous manipulation policies from large-scale egocentric human video, then grounds and refines those skills through robot post-training and human-in-the-loop correction.

The system combines an egocentric-video data pipeline, a unified robot stack, and a world-model-enhanced vision-language-action policy. Experiments cover more than 40 manipulation tasks, including failure recovery, generalization, and few-shot adaptation to long-horizon tasks.
